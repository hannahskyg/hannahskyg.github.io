Inspiring the Future Generation of Engineers
By Hannah Sky Gonzalez · November 2026

Teaching marginalized K-12 students Arduino, circuit design, and introductory data science concepts as an HDSI Fellow.

As a Fellow with the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute (HDSI) Lab 3.0 at UC San Diego, I've had the unique privilege of leading STEM outreach initiatives. Our core mission is to bridge educational disparities in local communities. To do this, we design and facilitate interactive workshops covering introductory circuits, Arduino microcontroller programming, and exploratory data analysis concepts for K-12 students.

Teaching technical subjects to younger audiences requires breaking down complex abstractions into hands-on analogies. Seeing a student write their first C++ conditional check to blink an LED or display sensor data on a small screen is incredibly rewarding. It shows that exposure and mentorship are key to building confidence in engineering fields early on.

In addition to active workshop instruction, I expanded our program reach by building an database of over 200 school administrators and PTA representatives across San Diego county. This outreach has successfully scaled our attendance numbers and secured new partnerships for upcoming semesters. For me, giving back through technical education remains one of the most meaningful aspects of my academic career.
