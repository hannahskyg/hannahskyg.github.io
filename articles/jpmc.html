Inside the JPMorgan Chase Data Science Desk
By Hannah Sky Gonzalez · June 2026

Building machine learning models for legal compliance and collaborating with agile engineering teams in Jersey City.

This summer, I joined JPMorgan Chase & Co. as a Data Science Analyst Intern. Working in the Jersey City office, I was tasked with developing an end-to-end machine learning solution designed to optimize legal compliance workflows. Specifically, my project focused on utilizing NLP techniques and predictive modeling to automate similarity detection across high volumes of complex legal documents, enabling faster reference lookups and reducing compliance latency.

Throughout the internship, I collaborated closely with cross-functional agile teams consisting of data scientists, software engineers, and product owners. This experience reinforced the importance of alignment between technical execution and broader business objectives. Translating statistical metrics like recall and F1-score into concrete operational time savings was crucial when presenting progress to stakeholders.

Beyond the codebase, my time in Jersey City and New York provided immense personal growth. Navigating a fast-paced corporate environment and working on models with real compliance implications was both challenging and incredibly rewarding. I look forward to carrying these lessons forward as I continue to explore machine learning applications in financial technology.
