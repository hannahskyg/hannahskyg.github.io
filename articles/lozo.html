UX Research in Shanghai: The LOZO Experience
By Hannah Sky Gonzalez · August 2025

Spending the summer conducting usability testing and developing UX personas for an AI-powered educational platform in China.

This summer, I took a leap outside of my comfort zone and moved to Shanghai, China, to intern as a Product Management and UX Research Intern at LOZO. LOZO is an educational technology company that develops AI-powered software to support personalized learning paths. My primary responsibility was conducting usability testing sessions with over 100 active platform users to locate friction points in the save-report and dashboard features.

By gathering behavioral data and tracking completion times, I compiled user journey maps and defined two distinct user personas. Integrating these persona insights directly into our wireframing and sprint cycles allowed the design and engineering teams to streamline critical flows. As a direct result, we improved platform report-saving efficiency by 50% for our user cohort.

Living and working in Shanghai was a transformative cross-cultural experience. Navigating a completely different language environment, understanding the local mobile-first ecosystem, and adapting to different corporate practices built my resilience and global product awareness. It proved that data-driven insights must always be paired with genuine empathy for user behaviors.
