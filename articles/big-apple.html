New York or... Somewhere?
By Hannah Sky Gonzalez · August 2025

A West Coast student's impressions of the East Coast pace, exploring Manhattan, and finding the best local matchas.

Before my internship began in Jersey City, I had only ever visited the East Coast briefly. Living and working right across the Hudson River from Manhattan was a complete shift in pace from San Diego. The immediate sensory overload of the subway systems, the sheer height of the skyscrapers, and the constant movement of crowds was both intimidating and exhilarating.

I quickly established a weekend ritual: hunting down the most unique matcha cafes in the Lower East Side and Soho. From traditional whisked Uji matchas to iced oat variants, I kept a running spreadsheet detailing their flavor profiles, whisk quality, and atmosphere. These weekend explorations became a grounding routine that helped me process the busy corporate environment of my weekdays.

Additionally, as a major basketball fan, finding local pickup games and visiting iconic street courts like West 4th Street (The Cage) was a highlight. Seeing the strategy and high-intensity plays in real-time mirrored the high energy of the city itself. This summer taught me how to adapt to a brand-new region, and I'm positive my East Coast adventures have only just begun.
