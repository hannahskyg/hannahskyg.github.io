Putting the US in Women in Business
By Hannah Sky Gonzalez · August 2025

Reflections on mentoring students, driving competitor analysis, and presenting consumer dashboards at UC San Diego.

Being a part of the Women in Business (WIB) organization at UC San Diego has defined much of my leadership journey on campus. Over the past year, I served as a Market Research Mentor and Intern. My primary focus was leading a consulting cohort of five student analysts through SWOT and competitor analyses, investigating Clarins' AI chatbot functionality and Spotify's AI personalization algorithms.

Through this program, we designed custom consumer surveys, gathered qualitative user datasets, and structured interactive visualization dashboards in Google Sheets. Helping junior students organize data into a coherent story—going from raw survey numbers to strategic business recommendations—allowed me to practice product strategy and coaching skills alike.

We ultimately presented our AI personalization recommendations to a group of over 100 students and advisors. This role highlighted the importance of fostering diverse perspectives in tech and business fields. Translating data science findings into client-ready consulting slides remains a key skill I'm excited to continue applying in my career.
