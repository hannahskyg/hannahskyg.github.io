Academic Spotlight
The Intersection of STEM and Business
By Hannah Sky Gonzalez · August 2025

How double majoring in Data Science and International Business at UC San Diego empowers me to turn analytical results into strategic action.

Double majoring in Data Science and International Business at UC San Diego has shaped my perspective on solving complex problems. Data Science provides me with the quantitative tools to build predictive models, run experiments, and clean complex datasets. However, statistics alone cannot drive change; they require context. International Business provides the frameworks to understand market incentives, product positioning, and cross-border operations.

Throughout my coursework at HDSI, I have focused on translating machine learning pipelines into actionable business insights. In my project work, I make it a priority to ask: "What business decision does this prediction support, and who is the user?" Bridging this gap has proved invaluable, whether mentoring teams in competitor analyses or presenting machine learning compliance tools during my internships.

As technology and global markets continue to merge, the demand for translators who can span both technical engineering and business strategy is higher than ever. I plan to continue utilizing this dual perspective to build data-driven, community-oriented solutions that address real-world challenges.
