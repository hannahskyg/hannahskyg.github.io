By Hannah Sky Gonzalez

Hi, I'm Hannah Sky Gonzalez! I'm a senior double majoring in Data Science and International Business at UC San Diego, graduating in April 2027. As a double major, I've developed a passion for connecting data with an international business mindset. When it comes to data science, I use my expertise to follow my curiosity, creating data science questions and taking them from a question to a solution that connects to impact through machine learning and AI. On campus, I'm involved with Women in Business, where I am a proud Latina in Tech, and work at a data science lab where I help inspire future engineers by teaching K-12 students Arduino and data science. I focus my work on mentorship and giving back to my community in any way that I can. For me, trying new things can be challenging, but I value opportunities that push me outside my comfort zone. As someone who interned in China, I learned to adapt to my surroundings and how to live in a different culture. Overall, my experiences have prepared me to work hard and passionately.

You must not let anyone define your limits because of where you come from.
— Remy, Ratatouille

Outside of my studies, I'm usually traveling to new countries, grabbing a matcha, or watching basketball. I probably already heard of the latest matcha cafe in the area. I'm a huge fan of college ball and March Madness. I believe the best insights come from having a mix of different experiences and perspectives. My internship at JPMorgan Chase & Co. has given me the opportunity to apply that mindset to real-world problems through machine learning and AI. Ultimately, I love to learn, teach, and inspire!
